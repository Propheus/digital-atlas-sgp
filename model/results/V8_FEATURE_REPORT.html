Building Density — The Singapore Story

Key insights from the 377K-building dataset:

🏙️ Most built-up
China Square (59%), Cecil (57%), Raffles Place (55%), Chinatown (51%). These are the CBD's densest floor-print neighbourhoods — more than half of every square meter is building.
🏢 Tallest
Central Subzone (Marina Bay): 70 floors, 245 m. 7 skyscrapers ≥40 floors. The only zone with genuine vertical concentration.
🏘️ HDB heartland
Tampines East: 345 HDB blocks, 421 high-rises ≥10 floors, 126K residents. The largest public housing estate in SGP.
🏭 Industrial kings
Jurong Island: 12,017 buildings, 3.7 km² footprint, 439 industrial. The petrochemical empire. Tuas extensions: 228-447 industrial each.
🏚️ Redev priorities
Top 5: Northshore (Punggol), Aljunied, Upper Thomson, Balestier, Taman Jurong — populated mature estates at 35-60% GPR utilization. Government has room to densify.
🏗️ Maxed out
Queenstown Margaret Drive, Holland Drive, Leonie Hill, Tiong Bahru Station — all at or above GPR capacity. Little room for more.
🌳 Data quality
29,935 buildings enriched with OSM heights (surveyed) via nearest-neighbor match. 10,431 HDB blocks authoritatively classified from government source. 188K remain ML-only classification.
🎯 For urban planners
Key ratio: gpr_utilization tells you where the Master Plan permits more building. Key warning: Some "utilization > 1.0" zones reflect buildings that exceed their plot's stated GPR — historic CBD lots with pre-2003 rights.

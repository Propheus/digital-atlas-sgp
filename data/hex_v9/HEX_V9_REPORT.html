Why Hex? Why Now?

Singapore's 332 URA subzones are politically meaningful but analytically uneven — Selegie (0.05 km²) and Central Water Catchment (68.6 km²) are both "one row" in the subzone table, a 1,300× size difference. Aggregating features at subzone level hides the real story: a 126,370-person HDB estate like Tampines East has specific streets that are well-served and specific corners that are underserved, but at subzone level they average out.

H3-9 hexagons (175m edge, ~0.12 km² each) are the natural unit for asking "can I walk to X in 10 minutes?". Every hex in Singapore is the same size, and every hex has its own population, buildings, transit access, and walkability score computed directly — not averaged from a much larger zone.

Key tradeoff: At hex level we get 21× more granularity than subzones but each hex has 21× less data. Features that are genuinely point-level (buildings, places, MRT/bus stops, traffic signals, hawker centres) become more accurate. Features that are polygon-level (census population, dwelling mix) are distributed dasymetrically using building footprints as weights, which introduces controlled error.

Known Limitations

⚠ MRT station count vs V8

80.9% match. V8 uses 1km radius buffer (double-counts); hex uses physical containment (truer). Not a bug, a semantic difference.

⚠ Bus stop count vs V8

42% match. Same buffer issue — V8 counts each stop multiple times across adjacent subzones; hex counts it once.

⚠ Persona distribution

NVIDIA personas are at subzone level; all hexes in the same subzone share identical persona values. Fine-grained distribution would need building-weighted redistribution.

⚠ HDB resale prices

Currently aggregated per planning area (town), distributed to all hexes in PA. Ideal: map each of 227K resale transactions to its block's hex.

⚠ Road segments

Speed bands mapped by midpoint only. A long road crossing 3 hexes only counts in 1. Better: split at hex boundaries.

⚠ Places near boundaries

88% of formal schools mapped. Some are just outside hex boundaries near water/reserves.

Use Cases Unlocked

🚇 MRT corridor planning

Identify exact blocks with >5K residents and >1km from MRT. Yishun East, Choa Chu Kang North, Pasir Ris West have 15+ such hexes.

🏥 Healthcare gap scan

CHAS clinic distribution is excellent — no populated hex lacks a clinic within 700m. This is a SGP success story.

🏫 School walk distance

Precise nearest-school distance per hex. Flag hexes where children walk >1km to formal school.

👴 Silver zone expansion

Cross elderly_count × walkability × silver_zones to find hexes with elderly residents but no traffic calming.

🏗️ Redevelopment blocks

Per-hex GPR utilization (future). Find specific 350m × 350m parcels under 50% built-up in high-GPR zones.

🚸 Pedestrian signal upgrades

Hexes with ped_standard > 5 and ped_elderly = 0 → candidates for "Green Man +" elderly-friendly retrofits.

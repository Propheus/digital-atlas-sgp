1 · What v5 is

v5 turns the atlas from a descriptive instrument (what is each hex) into a decision instrument (what would a new outlet capture, who can reach it, what does it cost, what is coming). Eleven validated layer families were added one at a time, each behind a five-part gate; every number in the master traces to a source, a validator, and a one-page validation entry. The release closes with a gated 256-d similarity embedding over the full feature set. Every claim in this report is reproducible from logs/validate_*.json.

4 · Validation: protocol, totals, and what it forced

Protocol (every layer, no exceptions): numbered machine checks → redundancy audit vs all existing columns (|r| > 0.9 ⇒ drop/redefine) → hand-reviewed archetype table → NaN/zone-type accounting (NaN ≠ 0, non-residential = Not-Applicable) → one-page signed entry in SITE_SELECTION_VALIDATION.md before merge.

Totals: 64/64 checks PASS across 12 layer validators. Nothing in the v5 S-layers shipped on a WARN (legacy core validators — buildings, roads, satellite, composites, road centrality — retain 8 historical WARNs that pre-date S1–S11).

5 · Findings ledger (new urban insights from v5 validation)

  • Yunnan p96 supermarket capture — the capture model re-derives the FairPrice desert from structure alone; Orchard p03 for cafés (saturation inversion works).
  • Cafés don’t follow offices in Singapore (lift 0.87) — they are heartland/mall creatures; bars follow bars (3.0), hotels pull bars (2.75), industry repels homes (0.50).
  • The bus city nobody sees: Yishun East reaches 190K people in 15 min on buses alone despite “rail-poor” metrics; breathing/interchange confusion resolved.
  • Per-exit footfall beats per-station: one Punggol-concourse or Novena exit out-foots any single Orchard exit (volume splits 13 ways).
  • The 45-min centre of gravity is Little India/Bendemeer (1.9–2.1M reach), not Raffles Place; Jurong East commands only the western shed (p70); Tuas labor pool p0.
  • Chinatown/Telok Ayer is the registration belt — 10.5K live entities even after winsorizing agent buildings; biz_per_address detects corporate-secretary towers (City Hall 109–131/address).
  • psf rents peak at new-build CBD (City Hall/Midtown era) and Nassim/Leedon, not the older large-format Orchard stock.
  • 245 km of covered linkways quantified — top-decile density in mature HDB towns; the most Singapore feature in the atlas.

8 · Known limitations & deferred

  • Commercial (shop) rent — Realis-only; rent_resi_psf_med is the documented gradient proxy.
  • BTO project-level locations — no longer openly published; town table + FAR-headroom allocation is the modeled estimate.
  • Huff λ is assumed (behaviorally prior-set, rank-stable) — not empirically identifiable from cross-sectional data.
  • OD is transit-only (single national mode-share constant); dorm private buses invisible.
  • Embedding deferred checks: per-hex Haiku explainability pass, leave-one-family-out, counterfactual-direction.
  • 5 legacy graph-embedding families (6 catalog entries: node2vec, gcn, super ×2, place2vec, mega) remain unavailable locally; GNN v3/v6 + plexis-e1 are synced — 7/13 catalog entries available.
  • Population vintage Jun-2024; consider re-anchoring to SingStat 2025 at the next data refresh.
